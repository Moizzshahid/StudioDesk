# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want participation in StudioDesk to be welcoming, constructive, and respectful, regardless of experience level or background.

## Expected behavior
- Be considerate and respectful.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's private information.
- Keep discussions professional and welcoming to beginners.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository owner privately through the contact options on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Moizzshahid). Please include a link or brief description of the incident and avoid reposting private information publicly.

Project maintainers will review reports as fairly and promptly as possible. They may remove or edit content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Maintainers will respect the privacy of the reporter and anyone affected to the extent reasonably possible. This project is maintained by an independent maintainer, so response times may vary.
